5 · Your checkup

Your checkup

Based on your answers: 3 things to fix, 1 way to save, and 1 question to ask.

Fix first

Your boat is barely covered. You said you have a boat but no boat policy. A home policy usually pays about $1,000 for boat damage. Get a boat insurance quote. ValuePenguin
Your life insurance ends with your job. Someone relies on your income, and your only coverage is through work. Price a term policy of your own. Life Happens
Your lawsuit protection looks low. You said you have more than $300,000 to protect and no umbrella policy. Ask what $1 million of umbrella coverage costs.

Could save

You may pay for towing twice. You have AAA. Many auto policies add roadside help too. Look for "roadside" on your auto bill and drop it.

Ask your agent

Does my home policy cover sewer backup? You own your home. Most policies leave sewer backup out unless you add it, and it's a common basement claim.
