# Coverage Check: screen-by-screen wording

Nov 7, 2026. Draft for review. Nothing here is built. Mark up anything: words, order, choices.

How to read this:

- **Q** is the question on screen. **Choices** are the tap answers. **Why** is the one-line "why we ask" under the question.
- **Shown when** says who sees a question. Anyone it doesn't apply to never sees it.
- Nothing is pre-selected anywhere.
- Every screen has a **Back** link and a plain progress bar. Single-answer screens move on as soon as you tap. Screens with more than one question have a **Continue** button.
- ⚠️ marks a choice that affects conversion. Those are collected at the end for you to decide one at a time.

Example used throughout: Minnesota homeowner, kids, a car, a boat, AAA, life insurance only through work, no umbrella. That person sees **12 screens**, and 8 of them take a single tap.

## 1. Start (also asks for your state)

**Headline:** Find the gaps and double-payments in your insurance.

**Subline:** About two minutes. No name, no email, no sales call.

**Three lines:**
1. Answer a few quick questions.
2. Tap the insurance you have.
3. Get a short list of what to fix and what to ask.

**Then:** You don't need your policies in front of you. Tap your state to start.

*(State map, then "Or pick from a list." Tapping a state starts the checkup.)*

**Small print:** General information, not insurance advice. We're not an agent or broker. Nothing you enter leaves this page unless you share it.

*The scattered policy icons stay above the headline.*

## 2. Own or rent

**Q:** Do you own or rent your home?
**Choices:** Own · Rent · Neither
**Why:** Owners and renters need different coverage.

---

## 3. Anyone relying on you

**Q:** Does anyone rely on your income?
**Sub:** A partner, kids, or a parent you help support.
**Choices:** Yes · No
**Why:** This decides whether life and disability coverage matter for you.

---

## 4. Car

**Q:** Do you have a car, truck, or van?
**Choices:** Yes · No
**Why:** If you drive, we'll check your auto policy.

---

## 5. Anything else with a motor

**Q:** Anything else with a motor?
**Sub:** Tap all that apply.
**Choices:** Boat · Jet ski · Motorcycle · RV · ATV · E-bike · None of these
**Why:** These are often less covered than people think.

*"None of these" moves on right away. Picking any others shows a Continue button.*

## 6. What you have ⚠️

**Q:** Which of these do you have?
**Sub:** Tap only the ones you're sure about.
**Why:** This is how we spot coverage you're missing or paying for twice.

| Choice | Shown when |
|---|---|
| Home insurance | You own |
| Renters insurance in my name | You rent |
| Renters insurance in someone else's name (like a roommate) | You rent |
| Flood insurance | You own |
| Earthquake insurance | You own, in a state with high earthquake risk |
| Umbrella policy (extra lawsuit protection) | Always |
| Life insurance through work | Always |
| Life insurance of my own | Always |
| Disability insurance (through work or my own) | Someone relies on your income |
| Health insurance | Always |
| AAA or another auto club | You have a car |
| None of these | Always |

*Auto insurance isn't on the list. Nearly every state requires it, so asking is a wasted tap.*

## 7. Savings

**Q:** Could you pay a $2,500 surprise bill from savings today?
**Choices:** Yes · No
**Why:** This tells us whether your deductibles fit your savings.
**Shown when:** You have a car, or home or renters insurance.

## 8. What you own

**Q (owners):** If you sold your home today and added your savings, would it come to more than $300,000?
**Q (everyone else):** Do you have more than $300,000 in savings and retirement accounts?
**Choices:** Yes · No · Not sure
**Why:** The more you own, the more a lawsuit could take.

## 9. Your auto policy

**Shown when:** You have a car.
**Heading:** Your auto policy
**Sub:** Answer from memory. "Not sure" is fine. It becomes a question for your agent.

**Q:** What's your collision deductible?
**Choices:** $500 or less · $1,000 · $2,000 or more · Not sure
**Why:** It's what you pay before insurance pays for damage to your car.

**Q:** Does your auto policy include roadside help or towing?
**Choices:** Yes · No · Not sure
**Why:** You have an auto club, which already includes it.
**Shown when:** You tapped AAA or another auto club.

## 10. Your home policy

**Shown when:** You tapped Home insurance.
**Heading:** Your home policy
**Sub:** Answer from memory. "Not sure" is fine. It becomes a question for your agent.

**Q:** What's your deductible?
**Choices:** $500 · $1,000 · $2,500 · $5,000 or more · Not sure
**Why:** It's what you pay before insurance pays on a claim.

**Q:** Does it cover water backing up from a drain or sewer?
**Choices:** Yes · No · Not sure
**Why:** Most policies leave it out unless you add it.

**Split:** Wherever this screen would have four or more questions, the last three (home value, hurricane deductible, plan) move to a second screen headed **A few more about your home**, with the sub "[Florida] has [hurricane] rules that change what you'd pay." ([Earthquake] in California, where the split also happens.)

**Q:** About what is your home worth?
**Choices:** $250,000 · $400,000 · $600,000 · $800,000 · $1 million or more · Not sure
**Why:** Some deductibles here are a percentage of your coverage, so we'll show them in dollars.
**Shown when:** States with hurricane or earthquake deductibles, and Florida.
*Coverage amount and market value aren't the same, so results always say "a home worth about $400,000" to make clear the dollar figure is an estimate built on the number you picked.*

**Q:** What's your hurricane or windstorm deductible?
**Choices:** 1% · 2% · 5% · 10% · A flat dollar amount · Not sure
**Why:** In [state], it's usually a percentage of your coverage, not a flat amount.
**Shown when:** Hurricane and windstorm deductible states.

**Q:** Is your home insured through [plan name]?
**Choices:** Yes · No · Not sure
**Why:** [Plan name] covers less than a regular policy.
**Shown when:** California (California FAIR Plan), Florida (Citizens), Texas (TWIA), and Louisiana (Louisiana Citizens). ⚠️

## 11. Your boat and other vehicles

**Shown when:** You picked anything on screen 5.
**Heading:** Your boat, motorcycle, and the rest *(lists what you picked)*

**Q (one row for each thing you picked):** Does your [boat] have its own insurance policy?
**Choices:** Yes · No · Not sure
**Why:** Home and auto policies cover very little of these.

**Q:** About what would it cost to replace your boat or jet ski?
**Choices:** $5,000 · $15,000 · $30,000 · $60,000 or more · Not sure
**Why:** We'll show how much of it a home policy wouldn't pay.
**Shown when:** You picked Boat or Jet ski and said it has no policy of its own, or weren't sure.

## 12. Your results

**Heading:** Your checkup
**Sub:** Based on your answers: [3 things to fix], [1 way to save], and [2 questions to ask].

**Groups, in this order:** Fix first · Could save · Paying twice · Ask your agent · Good to know *(collapsed)*

- The top five items show up front, highest priority first. The rest sit behind "Show [4] more."
- Empty groups don't appear.
- If nothing needs fixing: "Nothing urgent to fix. Here's what's worth knowing."

**Each item:**
- **Headline** (bold)
- **Why it applies to you** ("You said...")
- **What to do:** one action
- **Source** link, when the item states a fact about insurance or a law

**Buttons, three, equal size:** Share my list · Copy my list · Print my list
**Under the buttons:** Start over
**Small print:** General information, not insurance advice. We're not an agent or broker.

**What Share and Copy produce (plain text):**

### Dropped from today's version

- **Unsourced savings estimates**, like "typically saves $80 to $100 a year." They break the citation rule. Items 7, 9, 17, and 25 need a real source before launch. I'll find one for each during the build.
- **"10 times your income" for life insurance.** It's unsourced, and we don't ask income.
- **Every limit question:** liability limits, uninsured motorist limits, med pay limits, percent of income replaced. People can't answer these from memory. They now come back as "Ask your agent" items.
- **Annual premium questions.** No result used them.
- **The credit-card roadside choice.** Most card roadside is pay-per-use, so counting it as free was wrong. Only auto clubs count now.
- **"Most common" next to "2 vehicles."** It's an unsourced claim, and the question is now yes or no.
